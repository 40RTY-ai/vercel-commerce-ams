# collectionList

Two to four collection tiles — image and name — each linking to that collection in the store; for when the visitor is orienting ("what do you sell", "where do I start").

## When to Use

The visitor is orienting rather than choosing: "what do you sell", "where do I start", or a broad question where pointing at the right categories beats listing products.

## Don't Use When

The visitor asked for specific products — use `productGrid`.
