# comparisonTable

Two to four products side by side, with a one-line verdict and the pick highlighted — for when the visitor is choosing between specific options ("A or B?", "which is better for…", "what's the difference"). Each column shows the product's tile, price and add-to-cart; rows show what differs.

## When to Use

The visitor is choosing between specific options: "X vs Y", "which is better for…", "what's the difference between these". Also right after a `productGrid` when they narrow to a few.

## Don't Use When

They are still browsing — use `productGrid`. They want one recommendation — use `productDetail`. Only one product is in play.
